Hello,

JOSHIM AHMMED DIGANTA

Senior Video Editor & Broadcast Journalist

I’m Joshim Ahmmed Diganta, a professional Video Editor and Broadcast Journalist from Dhaka, Bangladesh, with 14+ years of experience in television, news, programs, documentaries and digital media. My career began in child journalism and media production, and I later worked with Ekushey Television, Sublime Media, Mohona Television and Nagorik Television. I specialize in video editing, broadcast production, visual storytelling and digital content. As a person with a disability, I have also been actively involved in disability rights, child rights and inclusive development through CSID. In 2023, I represented Bangladesh at the 10th International Abilympics in France, where I won 3rd place in ICT – Publishing. I am passionate about combining storytelling, technology and creativity to create meaningful visual content.
